**Problem:** Lost ID Cards on Campus

## 1. Problem Statement

Students at Babcock University lose their student ID cards regularly,
especially around high-traffic areas like hostels, the Game House at BUSA,
and the Stadium.

Currently, there is no central, searchable place to report or check for
lost items. Students have to ask around, check WhatsApp groups, or
walk to the Panels Office under the Amphitheatre to ask if their ID was found.

Because of this, many students never recover their IDs and are forced to
pay for a replacement, which costs money and time, and can affect their
access to hostels, exams, and other campus facilities.

Our solution is a simple Lost & Found platform where students can post
a lost item, post a found item, and search to see if their item has
already been found.

## 2. Research & Interviews

We interviewed 9 students who lost their ID cards on campus.

**6 Boys - Lost at Game House, BUSA:**
All 6 boys misplaced their ID cards at the Game House at BUSA. 
They were playing games, moving between tables, and putting their
cards down to pay or to keep their phones. By the time they were
leaving, the cards were gone.

4 of them later found their IDs at the Panels Office under the
Amphitheatre after asking around for days. 2 of them could not
find theirs at all and had to pay for a replacement.

**3 Boys - Lost at the Stadium:**
The other 3 boys lost their IDs at the Stadium during sports
activities and training. Their cards fell out of their pockets
while they were running and changing.

1 of them found his at the Panels Office. The other 2 did not
find theirs and had to pay for a replacement.

**What We Learned:**
1. ID Card is the most lost item on campus.
2. Game House and Stadium are the two highest-risk places because
students are active, moving, and not holding their cards.
3. Panels Office under the Amphitheatre is already acting as the
informal Lost & Found, but students only find out about it by
word of mouth.
